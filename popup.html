💡 Quick Guide: Portal Requirements FAQ

Passport Photos: Usually require 3.5 × 4.5 cm, 200–300 DPI, and 20–50 KB in JPEG format.

Digital Signatures: Usually 140 × 60 px, between 10–20 KB in JPEG or PNG.

Why DPI matters: Portals check DPI to prevent blurry printouts on admit cards & passports. DocFit injects official JFIF/EXIF resolution headers.

Privacy: All processing is done 100% on your laptop's memory. No data is stored or transmitted.
